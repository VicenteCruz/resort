First-24h Spa Deal: 50% Off Massages

Book your massage at the Princess Spa desk on Sunday 29 Nov (within 24 hours of check-in) to unlock the 50% welcome discount. Water circuit is 500 MXN.

À La Carte Evening Dress Code

Kotatsu, Bruschetta, Rodeo Grill (dinner), and Aroma require men to wear long trousers, closed-toe shoes, and sleeved shirts. Shorts are welcome at Yucatán Buffet.

Late Arrival Covered on 28 Nov

AC 1812 lands at CUN at 20:10 (~21:45 resort arrival). Yucatán Buffet serves late snacks 22:30–07:00, Plaza Bar is open 24h, and 24/7 Room Service is free.

"Stay at 1, Play at 3" Access

Your Platinum wristband grants access to neighboring Grand Riviera & Grand Sunset Princess restaurants, bars, and pools via the 15-minute internal shuttle train.

Playa del Carmen Weather Profile

Late November & early December starts Riviera Maya's dry winter season: expect warm highs around 28°C (82°F), gentle evenings near 22°C (72°F), and 27°C Caribbean water. Sunset is early (~17:15 EST), so schedule beach & cenote outings in the morning!
